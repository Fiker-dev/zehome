# TikTok content plan — Oct 2026

Growth is organic (no paid ads), so content is the sales engine. Format follows
the Bureau viral-ai-playbook (script → reference → CapCut), with one rule:

> **Real product footage only for the product.** AI video (CapCut AI, Pika) and
> Pexels clips are for mood/B-roll (a cosy bedroom, a gym, a dog on a walk).
> Never generate or use stock footage of a product we sell — buyers must see
> exactly what arrives. Buy one of each hero product from Perfect Dealz
> (≈ R900 for diffuser + Glow-Up Kit parts + massage gun) and film it.

## TikTok is a search engine too (SEO for TikTok)

- Say the keyword out loud in the first 3 seconds and put it in on-screen text
  ("flame diffuser", "ice roller", "pet hair remover"). TikTok indexes speech,
  captions and overlays.
- Caption = keyword sentence + price + "free delivery SA" + 3–5 hashtags.
- Bio link: https://www.zehomefinds.co.za (www — the apex redirects).
- Pin the three best performers. Reply to comments with video replies.

## Cadence (first 4 weeks)

- 1–2 posts a day, rotate products. Same product, new hook = new post.
- Post 18:00–21:00 SAST weekdays, 10:00–12:00 weekends (check your own
  analytics after week 2 and shift).
- Each script below has 3 hooks — film once, cut three versions.

## Repurpose every video

- Same clip → Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts + Pinterest Idea Pin (pin links
  to the product page = backlink and Pinterest search traffic).
- Turn the best comments into FAQs on the product page (products.json `faqs`).
- Monthly: compare views → sales (Google Sheet order log) per product; drop or
  replace products that get views but no sales (price/offer problem) or no views
  (hook/product problem). Use the `sa-product-research` skill before replacing.
